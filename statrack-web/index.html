Features

Powerful Statistics At Your Fingertips

Everything you need to track performance across seasons, matches, and profiles.

👤

Profile Management

Create up to 4 profiles per device. Track your children, yourself, or your team.

Max 4 profiles per device
📅

Season Organization

Organize data by seasons (2026/2027 format). Switch between seasons to view historical stats.

Automatic season detection
⏱️

Live Match Tracking

Real-time goal and assist counting with built-in timer. Short press +1, long press -1.

Haptic feedback included
🔒

End-to-End Encryption

All data encrypted with AES-256. Keys never leave your device. True privacy.

Military-grade security
🔄

Peer-to-Peer Sync

Synchronize data between devices without a server. Uses WebRTC and Bluetooth.

No cloud dependency
📱

Cross-Platform

Built with Flutter. Works on Android and iOS with identical features and design.

One codebase, two platforms
